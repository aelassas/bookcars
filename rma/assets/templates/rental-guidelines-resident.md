# RENTAL GUIDELINES — Resident (No Security Deposit)

Traffic Fines • Safe Driving • Rental Policy

## NO SECURITY DEPOSIT

Clients holding a valid Emirates ID and UAE Driving Licence may rent our vehicles without a security deposit, subject to the rental terms.

The rental agreement is completed electronically and confirmed through UAE PASS for identity verification and registration within the applicable TARS rental system.

## TRAFFIC FINES — PAYMENT WITHIN 24 HOURS

Any traffic or parking fine received during the rental period must be paid within 24 hours of notification.

If a fine is not paid within this period, continuation of the rental will only be possible after all outstanding fines have been fully settled.

## LARGE OR ACCUMULATED FINES

If the total amount of fines becomes significant or exceeds the value of the remaining prepaid rental period, the outstanding balance must be settled before the rental can continue.

## REPEATED LATE PAYMENT OF FINES

If a client repeatedly delays payment or accumulates unpaid fines, continuation of the rental may require a refundable security deposit of AED 1,500.

## SAFE DRIVING & SPEED LIMITS

Always follow the posted speed limit and all road signs.

In Abu Dhabi, there is no additional 20 km/h speed allowance. The speed displayed on the road sign is the maximum permitted speed.

## GPS MONITORING & RESPONSIBLE DRIVING

Our vehicles are equipped with GPS tracking. Serious or repeated speeding may result in termination of the rental agreement and return of the vehicle.

Our vehicles are powerful, but public roads are not a place to test maximum speed or vehicle performance.

Please drive responsibly, respect traffic regulations and pay all fines promptly.
